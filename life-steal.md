<h1>Guide For The Lifesteal SMP</h1>
to start off the name Lifesteal SMP implies you can steal other players hearts, which will be beneficial towards the players that gained them, but, there are certain conditions you need meet in order to get into the Lifesteal SMP Portal 
<h3>What are the requirements to go into the Lifesteal SMP Portal?</h3>
previously the developers had programmed it so a player must have the class E Rank in order to get in, but now, it has changed into 50 player kills for a player to get into the seperate Lifesteal SMP, and to avoid players farming with alternate accounts, the developers had also made a cooldown of 30 minutes for a player to be count as a player kill (Same-Victim)
