<h1>Guide For The Lifesteal SMP</h1>
to start off the name Lifesteal SMP implies you can steal other players hearts, which will be beneficial towards the players that gained them, but, there are certain conditions you need meet in order to get into the Lifesteal SMP Portal 
<h3>What are the requirements to go into the Lifesteal SMP Portal?</h3>
previously the developers had programmed it so a player must have the class E Rank in order to get in, but now, it has changed into 50 player kills for a player to get into the seperate Lifesteal SMP, and to avoid players farming with alternate accounts, the developers had also made a cooldown of 30 minutes for a player to be count as a player kill (Same-Victim)
<h3>What makes player kills a player kills?</h3>
the victim must have 24 hours playtime in order to become one of the players kills, or if the player doesn't have enough playtime, the player that killed the victim won't get the Player Kills count, not allowing the player to get to 50 player kills,
<h3>Why did this feature even added?</h3>
the purpose of making these cooldowns is to prevent players using alternates accounts to gain player kills easily such as 30 minutes same victim cooldown, and 24 hours playtime, 
<h3>Does the hearts have limit on how much players can have at a time?</h3>
yes infact it is now reduced to 22, so, before you hunt for another player, make sure you withdraw your hearts first, the command for withdrawing hearts = "/withdrawheart (amount)"
