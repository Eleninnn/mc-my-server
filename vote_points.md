<h1>Vote Points Guide</h1>

<h3>What are Vote Points?</h3>

Vote Points are a currency similar to Playerpoints and the usual money that is used in My-SMP, besides that votepoints are a type of currency with a higher value rather than the others,

<h3>What is Vote Points used for?</h3>

it is used to buy Plus Rank, it has many variations each being increasingly expensive from the first, on the GUI in '/vip', and could also be used to exchange into Playerpoints, by navigating to the /shop GUI and click Votepoints shop, there you will see an amethyst Icon where you can exchange 50 Playerpoints points per 1 VotePoints 

<h3>How to get Vote Points?</h3>

There's only one way to get Vote Points, which is to vote at a specific website, type in your name and vote, (**note:if you're a bedrock player, make sure to include the brx in your name**)
